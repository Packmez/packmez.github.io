Privacy Policy for Stillbit: ADHD Habit & Focus

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

PackMez ("we", "our", or "us") built the Stillbit: ADHD Habit & Focus app as a free, ad-supported application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Stillbit is designed as an offline-first application. All habits, focus sessions, preferences, and progress data you create are stored strictly locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal habit data on external servers, and the app does not require account registration.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the app free, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect and share certain data, including:

  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • App interactions (ad views and clicks)
  • Crash logs and diagnostics
  • Device or other IDs (such as the Google Advertising ID)

This data is encrypted in transit and used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Depending on your region, you may be presented with a consent dialog (Google UMP) to manage your ad personalization preferences.

You can review how Google uses data here: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
